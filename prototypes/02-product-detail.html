Investment Objective
The Fund aims to achieve long-term capital growth by investing primarily in equity securities of companies listed or traded in the Asia Pacific region (excluding Japan). The Fund will invest in companies which the Investment Manager believes to be undervalued and which have strong fundamentals and growth potential.
Investment Strategy
The Fund adopts a value-oriented, bottom-up investment approach. The Investment Manager seeks to identify companies trading below their intrinsic value with strong balance sheets, competent management and sustainable competitive advantages. The portfolio is concentrated in the Investment Manager's highest conviction ideas.
